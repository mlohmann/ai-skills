I built JobScout because I wanted a job-search workflow I could inspect and reuse.

I’m preparing an open-source release for ChatGPT and Claude. You bring your own profile, choose your job boards or start with a source list, and get a shortlist that shows the evidence behind each match. You can use the host assistant or optionally connect JEV for structured evaluations.

The engineering decisions were the interesting part: keeping personal data separate from shared instructions, applying hard filters before fit scores, preserving distinct job IDs, and keeping missing facts visible instead of turning them into confident answers.

I’ve also added an installation prompt you can give your AI agent, plus platform-specific packages and regression tests.
